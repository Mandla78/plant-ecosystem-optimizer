 MECHANICS.TXT  --  how the simulation works, and what we are unsure about

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. THE NUTRIENT CLOCK  (the single most important mechanic)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - Every cell starts with 100 nutrient points.
  - While a plant occupies a cell: -1 point per tick.
  - At 0 points the cell is uninhabitable, THE PLANT DIES, and the cell is left
    flagged as DEAD MATTER.
  - A cell with dead matter REGAINS +1 point per tick, capped at 100.
  - If a plant moves into a cell that has dead matter, that cell drains at only
    0.5 points per tick instead of 1.

  IMPLICATIONS:
  a) Maximum natural lifespan is ~100 ticks (or ~200 on a dead-matter cell that
     was allowed to refill, because of the halved drain).
  b) On a 500-tick level, a plant placed at tick 0 is long dead by scoring time.
     ONLY the last ~100-200 ticks of activity produce the scored final state.
  c) The garden is therefore a CHURNING system: colonise -> exhaust -> die ->
     regenerate -> recolonise. Steady-state occupancy will be well under 100%.
  d) Counter-measures that matter:
       Crystal Cactus     special: soil_nutrient_regeneration
       Whiteveil Mycelium special: nutrient_transfer_network (evens out nutrient
                          points across connected cells)
       Loamcrawlers animal: soil regen x1.05
       Grazeleths   animal: soil regen x1.15   <- the strongest one
     Keeping Grazeleths alive is worth real effort on L2/L3/L4.

  OPEN QUESTION: does the drain start at planting, or only at maturity? The
  spec says a plant "can only begin competing for nutrient capacity in a cell
  once it has matured", which hints drain may start at maturity. Worldtree
  (maturity 25) and Oak (20) would benefit a lot if so. UNRESOLVED - test.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. GROWTH AND SPREAD
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  time_to_maturity : ticks before the plant becomes "active". Before that it
                     does NOT spread, does NOT cast shade, does NOT trigger
                     area effects.
  spread_rate      : ticks between spread actions. LOWER = faster.
                     Fastest in the game: Crimson Vine, Razorgrass, Skyvine (1).
  spread_range     : radius of the pattern.
  spread_type      : VonNeumann (4-dir) / Moore (8-dir) / Row (horizontal line)
                     / Column (vertical line) / CrossHatch (multi-axis).
  preferred_soil   : the plant can only be PLACED ON or SPREAD INTO these soils.

  COMPETITION RESOLUTION (the spec is explicit, and slightly counter-intuitive):
  when two plants spread into the same cell, THE ONE THAT ARRIVES LAST WINS -
  neither is mature yet, so invasiveness_rank does NOT decide it. Rank only
  decides contests between established/mature plants.
  Exception noted in the spec: Ashroot Bramble (burnt soil only).

  MANUAL PLANTING always overwrites whatever is in the cell.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. SHADE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Casters (only once MATURE):
    Oak Tree           shade_radius 4   (maturity 20)  - huge footprint
    Purple Canopy Tree shade_radius 2   (maturity 15)
  Hurt by shade:
    Grass            no_shade_survival  - DIES in shade
    Dwarf Sunflower  no_shade_spread + adjacent_shade_penalty
  Require shade to live:
    Moonpetal Lily   shade_required
    Ghost Orchid     shade_required
  Overrides: can_grow_in_shade (no plant in this dataset actually has it).

  => Oak and Purple Canopy are BOTH weapons and hazards. A mature Oak sterilises
     a radius-4 disc for Grass. But Moonpetal Lily and Ghost Orchid CANNOT EXIST
     without that shade, and Ghost Orchid gates Starcap Colony.
  => Correct play: deliberate shade zones. Put the canopy trees together, grow
     Moonpetal / Ghost Orchid underneath them, keep Grass and Sunflower clear.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. SEASONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Purely deterministic, set by the level file. Only used to trigger rules.
    no_winter_spread : Rose Bush, Lavender  (cannot spread during winter)
    season_summer    : Orange Blossom gets spread_rate 4 (worse than its base 3)
  L3 and L4 END IN WINTER (tick 700+). See levels.txt.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. WORLD EVENTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Rain, Drought, Ash Eclipse, Earthquake. Scheduled in the level file. They are
  checked with "has transpired AT LEAST ONCE", so once the tick passes, the
  unlock stays satisfied forever. We never have to time anything to an event -
  we only have to wait for it.
    Drought     -> unlocks Crystal Cactus (with Grass coverage > 0.04)
    Rain        -> unlocks Mire Bloom     (with Blue Moss coverage > 0.05)
    Ash Eclipse -> unlocks Phoenix Bloom  (with Emberroot + Ashroot coverage)
    Earthquake  -> creates cracks. Only Skyvine (crack_spread) can cross them.
                   L4 only, at tick 700.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. SPECIAL RULES THAT CHANGE STRATEGY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  subsurface_growth (Whiteveil)     : coexists on a cell UNDER the main plant,
                                      and is PROMOTED to main plant when that
                                      plant dies. Free insurance against the
                                      nutrient clock - a Whiteveil carpet under
                                      everything means dying cells stay
                                      populated instead of going empty.
  coexist_all_species (Starcap)     : can share a cell with anything. The ONLY
                                      way to exceed one plant per cell. Good for
                                      both C and for diversity.
  resurrect_after_destruction       : Phoenix Bloom returns after removal.
  no_adjacent_plants (Living Topiary): will NOT spread into a cell that has any
                                      adjacent plant. Needs isolated open space -
                                      put it in a sparse region, or hand-place it
                                      on a checkerboard.
  die_if_isolated (Crimson Vine)    : must always have a neighbour. Plant in
                                      pairs or lines, never as singletons.
  die_if_neighbors_greater_than 4
  (Blue Moss)                       : dies if it has more than 4 neighbours. Do
                                      not let it get surrounded - keep Blue Moss
                                      in strips, not solid blobs.
  burnt_soil_radius 2 (Emberroot)   : converts surrounding soil to BURNT, which
                                      kills off nearly everything else. This is
                                      how we farm the 20 burnt cells that Ashroot
                                      needs - but it MUST be quarantined in a
                                      corner or it will eat the map.
  dead_matter_only_spread (Glowcap) : Glowcap spreads ONLY onto dead matter, so
                                      it needs us to kill things first. It
                                      thrives in the exhausted churning regions.
  Also: neighbor_spread_slowdown (Thornheart), adjacent_maturity_penalty
  (Glowcap, Bloodbloom), adjacent_maturity_boost (Blue Moss +1, Sunshard +2).

7. OPEN QUESTIONS - things we cannot settle from the spec alone
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Q1 Does nutrient drain start at planting, or at maturity?
  Q2 Is "coverage" divided by TOTAL cells or by PLANTABLE cells? The spec says
     "total available cell coverage", and the worked example uses total cells.
     MITIGATION: always overshoot every coverage gate so both readings pass.
  Q3 Does the scored lifespan reset when a subsurface plant is promoted?
  Q4 Exact CrossHatch geometry (diagonals only? both axes plus diagonals?).
  Q5 Do immature plants block a cell against another plant spreading into it?
  Q6 Is the JSON key "plant_index" or "index"? The full example uses
     plant_index, a smaller snippet uses index.
  These get resolved by SUBMITTING and reading the score back, not by theory.
