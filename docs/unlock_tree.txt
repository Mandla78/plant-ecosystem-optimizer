================================================================================
 UNLOCK_TREE.TXT  --  animals, gates, and the critical path
================================================================================

START: you always begin with exactly 5 plants -
   1 Grass, 2 Rose Bush, 5 Dwarf Sunflower, 6 Lavender, 12 Oak Tree
Everything else is locked. A planting action for a locked plant is IGNORED
(not an error), so it is always safe to try one speculatively.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ANIMALS ARE THE REAL GATEKEEPERS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Animals are not planted - they APPEAR when their requirements hold, and they
DISAPPEAR again when the requirements stop holding. 15 of the 26 unlock trees
need at least one animal present. On Level 1 animals_enabled = false, which is
exactly why Level 1 is stuck at 5 species.

CRITICAL: an animal leaving can RE-LOCK a plant you were relying on. Keep the
trigger populations alive all game; do not let them get overrun.

  ANIMAL        REQUIREMENT                                   EFFECT
  ------------  --------------------------------------------  -------------------
  Nectaris      Lavender cov >= 0.02  OR  group cov >= 0.02    Pollination spread
                of {Lavender, Rose Bush, Dwarf Sunflower,      x1.5, Flowering
                Orange Blossom}                                maturation x1.1
  Solwings      Dwarf Sunflower cov >= 0.03 AND                Seed dispersal x1.5
                Rose Bush cov >= 0.02                          on Flowering Seed
  Verdelopes    Grass cov >= 0.05                              Grass spread x1.1,
                                                               Rose maturation x0.9
  Loamcrawlers  Grass cov >= 0.04 AND Rose Bush count >= 10    soil regen x1.05
  Virexids      Lavender count >= 10 AND Grass count >= 10     shallow-root
                                                               spread x1.1
  Grazeleths    Grass cov >= 0.04 AND count >= 10 of           soil regen x1.15,
                {Rose Bush, Lavender, Orange Blossom}          Flowering
                                                               regression x2 (BAD)
  Canorals      Trees count >= 10 OR Flowering group cov       Seed plant
                >= 0.05 OR Worldtree count >= 1                spread_range x1.05
  Barkskips     Oak Tree count >= 8 OR Worldtree count >= 1    Tree spread x1.1
  Monocryx      any single species >= 50% dominance            none, BUT it
                                                               BLOCKS Amber Fern
  Rhizorends    count >= 25 of species_group                   none
                "Shallow-root Species"   <-- NAME BUG, below

THE EASY BLOCK: Nectaris, Verdelopes, Loamcrawlers, Virexids and Grazeleths ALL
trigger off nothing but Grass + Rose Bush + Lavender, which we have from tick 0.
Solwings just adds Dwarf Sunflower. So SIX of the ten animals are reachable in
the opening phase using only the starting plants. That is the entire early game:
carpet the map with the 5 starters, and six animals show up on their own.

THE NAME BUG: Rhizorends wants species_group "Shallow-root Species".
classifications.json spells it "Shallowroot Species" (no hyphen). If the engine
matches literally, Rhizorends NEVER appears, and Bloodbloom - gated solely on
Rhizorends - is unreachable. We assume 30 species and treat Bloodbloom as a free
lottery ticket: attempt it late, it costs nothing if it fails. The Virexids
EFFECT targets the same misspelled group, so that buff may silently do nothing
too. Harmless either way.

MONOCRYX IS A TRAP: let any species reach 50% and you lose Amber Fern. Since we
optimise for balanced proportions anyway this is nearly automatic - but watch
Grass and Razorgrass, the two fastest spreaders.

GRAZELETHS IS DOUBLE-EDGED: +15% soil regeneration (the best longevity effect in
the game) but ALSO regression_rate x2 on Flowering Plants, which is 10 of our
species. Worth keeping, but keep the flowering species topped up.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
UNLOCK WAVES  (computed by fixpoint over the dependency graph)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
LEVEL 1  -  5 / 31 species. NOTHING unlocks. No animals, no events.
            Do not spend a single minute on unlock logic here.

LEVEL 2  -  28 / 31.  Blocked: Crystal Cactus (no Drought), Phoenix Bloom (no
            Ash Eclipse), Bloodbloom (Rhizorends bug).
LEVEL 3  -  30 / 31.  Blocked: Bloodbloom only.
LEVEL 4  -  30 / 31.  Blocked: Bloodbloom only.

The wave structure is IDENTICAL on L2 / L3 / L4 - the bigger levels just add the
event-gated species. This is the build order:

  WAVE 0  (tick 0)      Grass, Rose Bush, Lavender, Dwarf Sunflower, Oak Tree
                        -> carpet the map to trigger the animal block

  WAVE 1  (animals up)  Blue Moss, Crimson Vine, Glowcap Fungus, Ironthorn
                        Shrub, Orange Blossom, Razorgrass, Stone Reed
                        (+ Crystal Cactus on L3/L4, once Drought has fired)

  WAVE 2                Mire Bloom (needs Rain), Moonpetal Lily, Purple Canopy
                        Tree, Silver Fern, Thornheart Bramble, Whiteveil
                        Mycelium

  WAVE 3                Amber Fern, Emberroot Tree, Ghost Orchid, Living
                        Topiary, Skyvine, Sporewood Tree, Sunshard Bloom

  WAVE 4                Ashroot Bramble, Starcap Colony, Worldtree Sapling

  WAVE 5  (L3/L4 only)  Phoenix Bloom (Ash Eclipse + Emberroot + Ashroot)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
THE CRITICAL PATH - the longest chains, start these first
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Grass + Rose -> Loamcrawlers -> Blue Moss -> Silver Fern -> Amber Fern
  Grass + Rose -> Loamcrawlers + dead matter -> GLOWCAP FUNGUS
               -> Whiteveil Mycelium (Glowcap cov > 0.05)
               -> Sporewood / Emberroot / Starcap
               -> Ashroot Bramble -> PHOENIX BLOOM
  Lavender + Blue Moss -> Moonpetal Lily -> Ghost Orchid -> Starcap Colony
  Oak x6 + Purple Canopy x6 + Sporewood x4 + Barkskips -> WORLDTREE SAPLING

  FOUR BOTTLENECKS TO START EARLY:

  1. GLOWCAP needs DEAD MATTER (feature > 0.05) and it only spreads onto dead
     matter. Dead matter appears only when plants die, which takes ~100 ticks of
     nutrient drain. So build a SACRIFICIAL ZONE at tick 0: a dense block of
     Grass that is allowed to starve around tick 100, manufacturing exactly the
     dead matter Glowcap needs. Nothing else in the game produces it on demand.

  2. PURPLE CANOPY TREE (maturity 15) gates Skyvine (x3), Emberroot (x4),
     Sporewood (x4), Living Topiary (x3) and Worldtree (x6). Get 6+ planted the
     moment Blue Moss + Crimson Vine coverage allows.

  3. OAK TREE (maturity 20, spread_rate 7 - the slowest thing we need 8 of, for
     Barkskips -> Worldtree). Oak is UNLOCKED FROM TICK 0, so there is no reason
     to wait: hand-plant all 8+ Oaks in the very first ticks.

  4. PHOENIX BLOOM needs Ashroot coverage > 0.04, and Ashroot only grows on
     BURNT soil. On L4 that is 2,401 burnt cells. Each Emberroot burns a radius-2
     disc (~25 cells), so that implies roughly 100 well-spread Emberroots, all
     quarantined away from everything else. This is a real sub-project - on L4 it
     may be cheaper to accept losing Phoenix Bloom than to burn 4% of the map.
     On L3 it is 901 cells (~36 Emberroots) which is more affordable.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
NOTE ON THE BIG LEVELS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Look at the L4 column above. A 0.05 coverage gate is 3,000 cells, and we get
only 16,000 manual placements in the ENTIRE game. Several such gates at once is
far beyond hand-planting. On L3 and L4 the unlock chain can only be driven by
NATURAL SPREAD - we place seed clusters and let them expand. On L2 (7,000 cells)
a 0.05 gate is only 350 cells, so L2 can be driven much more directly by hand.

AN ASSUMPTION WE NEED TO TEST (do not treat as fact):
Is an unlock PERMANENT once its condition has been met, or is it re-evaluated
every time we try to plant? The spec only says this explicitly for events ("has
transpired at least once"). For coverage gates it is unstated.
  - If unlocks are permanent, we can let a booster species boom, trip the gate,
    then let it fade back so it does not distort the final entropy. Much easier.
  - If they are re-evaluated live, every booster population must be HELD at its
    threshold for as long as we still want to plant the species it gates.
SAFE PLAY until we know: hold the booster populations at threshold rather than
letting them collapse, and do all the planting of a newly unlocked species while
its gate is still comfortably satisfied. That works under both readings.
