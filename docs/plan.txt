--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
GOVERNING PRINCIPLE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Get a VALID, SCORING submission on the board for every level EARLY, then improve
it. A mediocre submitted solution beats a brilliant unsubmitted one. Every level
gets a safe baseline before anyone touches tuning.

Second principle: prefer strategies that do NOT depend on simulating spread
exactly. We cannot verify our simulator against theirs. So we lean on:
  (a) clearing every unlock gate with a wide margin, and
  (b) dense manual seeding in the final ~100 ticks,
both of which still work even if our spread model is somewhat wrong.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
THE SHARED TEMPLATE (all levels)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Because scoring only looks at the final tick, and plants only live ~100 ticks,
every level has the same three phases:

  PHASE A - ESTABLISH (tick 0 -> ~40%T)
    Carpet with the 5 starters to trigger the 6 easy animals. Hand-plant Oaks
    immediately (slowest maturity). Start the sacrificial Grass block that will
    become the dead matter Glowcap needs.

  PHASE B - UNLOCK CASCADE (~40%T -> ~80%T)
    Walk the wave list in unlock_tree.txt. Each time a species unlocks, seed it
    into its assigned territory. Keep booster populations at threshold.

  PHASE C - FINAL COMPOSITION (last ~100 ticks - THE ONLY PART THAT SCORES)
    This is where the score is actually made. Use all 20 plants/tick to:
      - top up any species that is thin (every species present raises H),
      - refill cells emptied by the nutrient clock,
      - level the histogram so no species dominates,
      - keep everything under 50% (Monocryx).
    Plant as EARLY within this window as possible so survivors are older
    (longevity), but late enough that they are still alive at tick T.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
LEVEL 1 - GREENHOUSE  (50x50, 500 ticks, 1,800 plantable, 5 species, no animals)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
This is a pure packing problem, and it is the cheapest score on the board. Do it
first.

  Ceiling: H = log_31(5) = 0.4687. Nothing can change that.
  So the ENTIRE game here is maximising C and keeping the 5 species balanced at
  20% each.

  Layout: partition the 1,800 plantable cells into 5 territories of ~360 each.
  Constraints that shape the partition:
    - Oak (shade radius 4, matures at tick 20) KILLS Grass in its disc and stops
      Dwarf Sunflower spreading. Put Oak in a corner, far from Grass/Sunflower.
    - Rose Bush and Lavender cannot spread in winter (ticks 300-399). Their
      expansion must happen before 300 or after 400.
    - Grass is the fastest filler (maturity 1, spread 2) - use it to mop up
      leftover space, but cap it well under 50%.
    - Clay beds (360 cells) are unusable - no starting plant accepts clay.
      Ignore them entirely.

  Timing: because nothing lives past ~100 ticks, the tick-0 planting is purely
  to seed the system. The population that actually gets SCORED is whatever is
  established from roughly tick 380 onward. Plan a heavy replant sweep across
  ticks ~390-499 (110 ticks x 20 = 2,200 placements, more than the 1,800
  plantable cells - so we can literally hand-place the entire final garden).

  THAT IS THE KEY L1 INSIGHT: we have more end-game placements than plantable
  cells. L1 does not need good ecology at all - it needs a well-chosen final
  hand-painted picture, with spreading as a bonus on top.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
LEVEL 2 - GARDEN  (70x100, 500 ticks, 6,135 plantable, 28 species)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The sweet spot: big enough for real spreading, small enough that gates are cheap
(a 0.05 gate is only 350 cells).

  Target H ~0.95+ with all 28 reachable species present.
  Blocked: Crystal Cactus (no Drought), Phoenix Bloom (no Ash Eclipse),
  Bloodbloom (Rhizorends name bug).

  Run the wave schedule. Rain lands at tick 250, which opens Mire Bloom - and
  Mire Bloom is the only plant that takes clay, so it gets the lake rings
  (166 clay cells) all to itself, cells nothing else can use.

  Endgame budget: last 100 ticks = 2,000 placements against 6,135 plantable
  cells, i.e. we can hand-place about a third of the final garden. Enough to
  guarantee every species is represented and to fix the balance, but the bulk
  must come from spread.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
LEVEL 3 - PARK  (150x150, 800 ticks, 18,295 plantable, 30 species)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
All events land early (Drought@10, Rain@150, Ash Eclipse@300), and 800 ticks is
plenty of room for the full chain. This should be our best H.

  Watch out: a 0.05 gate is 1,125 cells - already past what we can hand-plant.
  Natural spread must carry the unlock chain. Seed clusters, do not paint.

  Emberroot quarantine: we need burnt soil for Ashroot (feature >= 20) and then
  Ashroot cov > 0.04 = 901 cells for Phoenix Bloom. That means ~36 Emberroots,
  placed in a corner block, deliberately walled off from the rest of the map by
  distance so their radius-2 burn does not sterilise our other territories.

  THE LEVEL ENDS IN WINTER (tick 700-799). Rose Bush and Lavender cannot SPREAD
  then. Establish them before 700, and top them up by hand at the end
  (hand-planting is not spreading, so it should still be allowed - verify).

  Shade districts: put the Purple Canopy / Oak cluster together and grow
  Moonpetal Lily and Ghost Orchid underneath. Ghost Orchid gates Starcap Colony,
  which is the one species that can coexist on occupied cells - free C and free
  diversity, so it is worth real effort.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
LEVEL 4 - FOREST  (200x300, 800 ticks, 54,012 plantable, 30 species)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The hard one, and it is hard for one specific reason: 16,000 total placements
against 54,012 plantable cells. We can touch at most 30% of the map by hand, and
a single 0.05 gate is 3,000 cells.

  => L4 is won by SEED GEOMETRY, not by planting volume. Place many small
     clusters early, spaced so their spread fronts meet just about at the end,
     and let exponential growth do the filling.
  => Spend the manual budget on (1) the unlock chain and (2) the final-100-tick
     diversity top-up, not on bulk coverage.
  => Phoenix Bloom needs 2,401 Ashroot cells on burnt soil - roughly 100
     quarantined Emberroots, burning ~4% of the map. Judgement call: that may
     cost more coverage than the +1 species is worth. Decide with the simulator;
     if it is close, SKIP Phoenix Bloom on L4 and take 29 species.
  => Earthquake at tick 700 creates cracks. Only Skyvine crosses them. Have
     Skyvine established before 700.
  => Also ends in winter - same Rose/Lavender caveat as L3.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
BUILD ORDER AND TIMETABLE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  11:00 - 11:35   Simulator + scorer. Grid, nutrients, maturity, the 5 spread
                  patterns, soil rules, unlock trees, animals, entropy and
                  longevity scoring. Good enough to compare strategies, not
                  perfect.
  11:35 - 11:55   L1 solver (territory partition + endgame paint).  SUBMIT L1.
  11:55 - 12:40   Wave-driven planner for L2.                       SUBMIT L2.
  12:40 - 13:20   Apply the same planner to L3 and L4.              SUBMIT BOTH.
  13:20 - 14:30   Iterate. The leaderboard is our only instrument for the
                  unknown a and k - compare our own score across variants.
  14:30 - 15:00   Final resubmissions and buffer. Stop touching things at 14:50.

  Each submission needs a ZIP (code) plus the JSON. Keep the generator
  deterministic and reproducible - rule 6 requires the same output for the same
  input, so no unseeded randomness anywhere.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
RISKS AND HOW WE HANDLE THEM
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  R1 Our simulator disagrees with theirs.
     -> Never rely on precise timing. Overshoot every gate. Lean on the endgame
        hand-planting, which is deterministic regardless of spread behaviour.
  R2 Wrong JSON key (plant_index vs index).
     -> First submission is the test. If L1 scores zero, that is the first
        thing to check.
  R3 Coverage denominator is plantable cells, not total cells.
     -> Overshoot every threshold so both readings pass.
  R4 An animal disappears and re-locks a species mid-run.
     -> Hold booster populations at threshold instead of letting them collapse.
  R5 We run out of time.
     -> That is why L1 ships first and every level gets a baseline before any
        tuning. Worst case we submit four mediocre-but-valid solutions.
